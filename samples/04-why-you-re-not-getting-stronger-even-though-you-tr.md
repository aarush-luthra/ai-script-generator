# why you're not getting stronger even though you train every day

**Niche:** Fitness · **Target:** 45s · **Tone:** blunt · **Model:** gpt-6-luna

## Script

**[0:00–0:05] HOOK**

If your first set is a grinder, you're sacrificing reps you need to build.

**[0:05–0:43] BODY**

On bench, I took the first work set near failure every day. I’d get 8 reps, then 5 on the next set, even at the same weight. My warm-ups started feeling heavy. I was testing myself, not building strength.

Keep about two reps in reserve on most sets. Choose a range, say 6 to 10, and log the load and reps after every session. When every set reaches 10 with clean form, add the smallest weight jump next time. Then start near 6. That gives you a target without retesting your limit every workout.

**[0:43–0:47] CTA**

Comment: did your bench reps hold across all your sets?
